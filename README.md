# Kućerak 111 - Holiday House Promotional Website

## 🇬🇧 English

Official promotional and informational website for the **"Kućerak 111"** holiday accommodation, located in Banja Vrdnik on the slopes of Fruška Gora. This project was developed as a real-world solution to showcase property capacity, outdoor amenities, and provide direct guest booking inquiries.

### 🌟 Key Features
* **Modern UI/UX Design:** Built using *Tailwind CSS* with a customized color palette (beige, olive, and earthy tones) tailored to the natural mountain environment.
* **Bilingual Support (Serbian / English):** Fully adapted for both local and international visitors (featuring `index.html` and `en.html` pages).
* **Interactive Components & Galleries:**
  * **Modals (Popup windows)** for detailed specifications of each apartment (Blue, Green, and Yellow) with an integrated multi-image switching system.
  * **Auto-sliding Carousel** with seamless translation logic and an interactive **Lightbox** gallery showcasing the exterior and yards.
* **Booking & Inquiry System:**
  * Integrated **Web3Forms** service for direct email inquiry delivery without requiring a custom backend.
  * **Client-Side Validation:** Disables past date selections, automatically updates minimum check-out dates, and dynamically validates phone number formatting.
  * **Spam Protection & Rate Limiting:** Built-in honeypot anti-spam fields and a 5-minute cooldown restriction between consecutive inquiry submissions.
* **Interactive Map:** Embedded Google Maps location (Vojvođanske brigade 93, Vrdnik).

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **HTML5** & **JavaScript (ES6+)** – Core structure and client-side logic (modals, sliders, validation, and local storage management).
* **Tailwind CSS** (via CDN) – Responsive styling and mobile-first layout design.
* **FontAwesome (v6.4.0)** – Vector icons for enhanced UI elements.
* **Google Fonts** (*Plus Jakarta Sans* & *Playfair Display*) – Typography and brand identity.
* **Web3Forms API** – Secure form submission handling.

### 🚀 Running Locally
1. Download or clone this repository to your computer.
2. Open the project folder in **Visual Studio Code**.
3. Install the **Live Server** VS Code extension.
4. Right-click on `index.html` and select **Open with Live Server**.

## 🇷🇸 Srpski

Zvanični promotivni i informativni veb-sajt za turistički smeštaj **"Kućerak 111"**, smešten u Banji Vrdnik na obroncima Fruške gore. Projekat je razvijen kao samostalno rešenje iz realnog sveta sa ciljem da posetiocima predstavi smeštajne kapacitete, prateće sadržaje i omogući direktnu rezervaciju.

### 🌟 Ključne karakteristike
* **Moderan UI/UX dizajn:** Izrađen uz pomoć modernog *Tailwind CSS* okvira sa prilagođenom paletom boja (bež, maslinasti i zemljani tonovi) prilagođenom prirodi i planinskom ambijentu.
* **Dvojezična podrška (Srpski / Engleski):** Potpuno prilagođen domaćim i stranim turistima (stranice `index.html` i `en.html`).
* **Interaktivne komponente i galerije:**
  * **Modali (Popup prozori)** za detaljan pregled specifičnosti svakog apartmana (Plavi, Zeleni i Žuti) sa integrisanim sistemom za prebacivanje slika.
  * **Automatski karusel (Slider)** sa prilagođenom logikom klizanja i interaktivna *Lightbox* galerija za prikaz eksterijera i dvorišta.
* **Sistem za rezervacije i upite:**
  * Integrisan servis **Web3Forms** za direktan prijem upita u inbox bez potrebe za sopstvenim bekendom.
  * **Klijentska validacija datuma i telefona:** Onemogućavanje izbora datuma u prošlosti, automatsko prilagođavanje minimalnog datuma odjave i dinamička provera formata broja telefona.
  * **Zaštita od spama (Honeypot) i Rate Limiting:** Ugrađena zaštita od botova i vremensko ograničenje (5 minuta) između uzastopnih slanja upita kako bi se sprečile zloupotrebe.
* **Interaktivna mapa:** Ugrađena Google Maps lokacija (Vojvođanske brigade 93, Vrdnik).

### 🛠️ Korišćene tehnologije
* **HTML5** & **JavaScript (ES6+)** – Struktura i klijentska logika (upravljanje modalima, sliderima, validacijama i lokalnom memorijom).
* **Tailwind CSS** (kroz CDN) – Dizajn korisničkog interfejsa i respozivnost (Mobile-first pristup).
* **FontAwesome (v6.4.0)** – Ikonice za vizuelno obogaćivanje interfejsa.
* **Google Fonts** (*Plus Jakarta Sans* & *Playfair Display*) – Tipografija i vizuelni identitet brenda.
* **Web3Forms API** – Sigurno prosleđivanje mejl upita sa kontakt forme.
