Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your stay at Kućerak 111

Can more than 12 people attend a celebration or team building event?

The sleeping capacity across all three apartments is 12 people in total. However, for daytime events, birthdays, and team building gatherings, the complex can accommodate a larger number of guests. Stays exceeding the overnight capacity (12+ people) are subject to an additional charge by prior arrangement.

Are you pet-friendly?

Yes, we are pet-friendly! Your well-behaved and house-trained pets are more than welcome. Please notify us in advance when sending your inquiry so we can prepare everything for your arrival.

What is the minimum stay requirement?

The minimum stay in our apartments is 2 nights. For longer stays (5 or more nights), we automatically apply a 15% discount to your total booking amount.

When is the swimming pool open?

The outdoor pool is open during the summer season (approximately from May to October, depending on weather conditions). It is regularly maintained and filtered to ensure the water is always perfectly clean for our guests.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is available from 2:00 PM, and check-out is by 10:00 AM on the final day of your stay. If you require different timing due to event organization, please feel free to state it in the notes section.

Does the property provide Wi-Fi and air conditioning?

Absolutely! All our apartments are fully air-conditioned, and the entire complex (including the yard and summer house) is covered by fast, free Wi-Fi, allowing you to enjoy your stay seamlessly.
